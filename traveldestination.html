Mt. Pulag National Park

Mt. Pulag is the third-highest mountain in the Philippines, famous for its breathtaking sunrise, cool climate, and the stunning "Sea of Clouds." It is one of the country's most popular hiking destinations.

Mt. Pulag Summit

Reach the summit and witness an unforgettable sunrise above the clouds.

Sea of Clouds

Experience the magical blanket of clouds surrounding the mountain peaks.

Camping Area

Enjoy camping under the stars with cool mountain air and beautiful scenery.
